Case Study 1: Fellow Ode Launch

Eight Ounce Coffee · 2020

Supported the launch of the Fellow Ode Brew Grinder across editorial, e-commerce, email, and social media, combining product storytelling with manufacturer coordination and staff training.

Product Coordination: Participated in pre-launch calls with Fellow's founder and engineering team in San Francisco to coordinate launch details, resolve outstanding questions, and address potential issues ahead of release.

Editorial & E-commerce: Wrote a long-form blog feature exploring the grinder's design, influences, and technical capabilities, alongside product description copy on the wholesale and retail site highlighting its features and value proposition.

Email & Social: Developed promotional copy for launch communications across email and social media, maintaining consistent product messaging across channels.

Staff Training: Trained team members to operate the grinder, building product knowledge and preparing staff to demonstrate its features and assist customers.

Impact: Contributed to a coordinated, multi-channel launch connecting manufacturer collaboration, product marketing, and staff education, driving impressive sales in the first month.
